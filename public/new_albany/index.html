Advice

  • Post Code on Github
    • It can be terrible code!
    • It can be buggy!
  • Don't be afraid of "second rate" companies
  • Show your personality
  • Don't pretend you know something you don't
  • Play to your strengths

Starting out

  • Codebases at companies are messy
  • There's a lot you don't know
  • Be ambitious but not stupid
  • Don't listen to old, grumpy coworkers
  • Watch talks, read blogs, learn on your own
